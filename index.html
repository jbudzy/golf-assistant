🌬️ Wind Rules
  • Headwind: +10 yds per 10 mph • Ball balloons; favor lower, punchier shots (P2/P3).
  • Tailwind: -7 yds per 10 mph • Flatter flight, less backspin, extra rollout.
  • Crosswind: +3–5 yds drag loss • Aim into wind or hit a piercing low punch.
⛰️ Elevation Rules (1y per 3ft)
  • Uphill: +1 yd per 3 ft rise (+15ft → +5y, +30ft → +10y) • Releases more.
  • Downhill: -1 yd per 3 ft drop (-15ft → -5y, -30ft → -10y) • Lands softer.
  • Key Cue: "Breezy? Swing it easy." Add/subtract yards directly in Target Distance.
