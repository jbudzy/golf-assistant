Tour Feel Routine
  • 1
    Look-at-the-hole rehearsals: Take 2 fluid practice strokes while looking directly at the cup. Let your eyes feed distance to your hands.
  • 2
    Soft grip pressure (3/10): Feel the weight of the putter head swinging like a free pendulum. Don't steer or decelerate.
  • 3
    One tempo, variable length: Trust the 76 BPM beat. For longer putts, simply let the arms swing wider back and through at that same tempo.
  • 4
    Look-and-go (≤ 2 seconds): Step in, take one last glance at the cup, look down, and trigger the stroke immediately while the visual image is vivid.
